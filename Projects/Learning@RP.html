About This Project

Technology Used:
• Figma (UI/UX design & prototyping)
• Icons8 (icons and illustrations)
• Unsplash (course and lesson images)

Features:
• Short video lessons and structured mini-courses
• Course categories and course overview pages
• Lesson details with progress tracking
• Interactive elements such as toggles, overlays, and loading spinners
• Clean and RP brand-inspired UI design

Target Audience:
• RP students interested in learning new skills
• Students exploring topics beyond their diploma modules
• Learners who prefer short, flexible, mobile-friendly lessons

Challenges Faced:
• Designing a consistent UI kit without using pre-made templates
• Ensuring clear navigation without overloading the interface
• Balancing an academic look with a modern, student-friendly design
• Creating realistic interactions within a prototype environment
